# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, offer constructive feedback, accept corrections gracefully,
and focus discussion on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, threats, and
unwelcome contact are not acceptable. Public or private communication connected to the project is
covered by this policy.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they consider inappropriate. Report concerns privately to the project
maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as
possible.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
